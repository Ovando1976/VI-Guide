# Heritage Image Research Pass 02

Updated: 2026-07-21

## Scope

This pass focuses on image leads and profile-support material for the first three elected governors of the U.S. Virgin Islands:

- Melvin H. Evans
- Cyril E. King
- Juan F. Luis

The goal is not simply to find a visible portrait. The goal is to locate the strongest reusable image source, record rights and attribution requirements, and identify contextual images that can support each governor's profile, timeline, and map connections.

### Contextual image opportunities

1. Howard University or Howard College of Medicine archival material connected to Evans's medical training.
2. Virgin Islands Department of Health historical material connected to his service as Health Commissioner from 1959 to 1967.
3. Congressional portrait or official federal image connected to his service as Delegate to Congress from 1979 to 1981.
4. Diplomatic or State Department image connected to his appointment as U.S. Ambassador to Trinidad and Tobago.
5. St. Croix location imagery connected to Christiansted and his public-health career.

### Editorial significance

Evans should be presented as more than the first elected governor. His profile should connect medicine, territorial public health, elected government, congressional representation, agriculture, fisheries, and diplomacy.

### Contextual image opportunities

1. Governor Juan F. Luis Hospital and Medical Center.
2. Schneider Regional Medical Center.
3. Myrah Keating Smith Community Health Center.

### Editorial significance

Luis's profile should connect the governor directly to the territory's healthcare infrastructure. The hospital and clinic network provides a strong places-and-people storyline across St. Croix, St. Thomas, and St. John.

## Portrait project lead

Government House documented a project to create and display portraits of all elected governors. The 2018 unveiling of portraits of Roy L. Schneider and Charles W. Turnbull confirms that Government House is the highest-priority institutional contact for the full governors collection.

- Source: https://www.vi.gov/official-portraits-of-former-governors-unveiled/

The article states that original portraits were intended for Government House, St. Thomas, with prints displayed at Government House locations in Christiansted and Frederiksted and at the Battery on St. John.

## Rights and curation decisions

- NGA biography portraits remain research leads, not approved production assets.
- Government House should be asked for either digital official portraits or written permission to reproduce portrait photography or paintings.
- Paintings require both image-source permission and attention to the artist's copyright.
- Named airports, hospitals, schools, and clinics are contextual images and should not replace a primary portrait.
- Every downloaded image should retain creator, institution, date, source URL, rights statement, and credit line.
