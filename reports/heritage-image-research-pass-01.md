## Curation rules reinforced by this pass

1. A publicly visible image is not automatically reusable.
2. Public-domain and CC0 images should still retain an institutional credit line and source URL.
3. A contextual object or place can strengthen a profile but must not replace a missing portrait without clear labeling.
4. Wikimedia uploads require provenance review when the depicted work predates the uploader's claimed creation date.
5. Local family, school, church, hospital, and community archives should be treated as primary acquisition partners for the Unsung Heroes collection.
