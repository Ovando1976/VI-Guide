# Heritage Image Acquisition Plan

Updated: 2026-07-21

## Purpose

Move the heritage image program from research leads to verified, permission-cleared, app-ready assets for governors, unsung heroes, institutions, documents, and connected places.

## Immediate acquisition priorities

### Priority A — complete elected-governor portrait series

Primary institutional target: Government House, U.S. Virgin Islands.

Reason: Government House publicly documented a territory-wide project to commission portraits of all elected governors, with originals and prints intended for Government House locations and The Battery on St. John.

Primary source lead: U.S. House of Representatives, History, Art & Archives.

Requested package:

## Government House permission request template

Subject: Request to reproduce official portraits of elected U.S. Virgin Islands governors in VI Guide

Dear Government House Communications Team,

VI Guide is developing an educational, territory-centered heritage experience that will help residents, students, visitors, and families explore the people and places that shaped the U.S. Virgin Islands.

We are requesting permission to reproduce the official portraits of the territory's elected governors in a non-exclusive digital format within VI Guide. Government House previously documented a portrait project intended to preserve and present the history of the territory's elected governors, and we would like to reflect that history accurately and respectfully.

We are requesting the highest-resolution approved image available for each of the following governors:

- Melvin H. Evans
- Cyril E. King
- Juan F. Luis
- Alexander A. Farrelly
- Roy L. Schneider
- Charles W. Turnbull
- John P. de Jongh Jr.
- Kenneth E. Mapp
- Albert Bryan Jr.

For each image, please provide or confirm:

1. the approved image file;
2. the artist or photographer;
3. the official title and date, if known;
4. the preferred credit line;
5. permission terms for display in a public-facing web and mobile application;
6. whether proportional crops and responsive derivatives are permitted;
7. any restrictions on commercial, educational, or promotional use.

VI Guide will preserve the supplied attribution, source, rights status, and provenance with every image record. We will not imply endorsement by Government House, and we will remove or revise any image promptly if requested by the rights holder or issuing institution.

We would also welcome approved contextual photographs that show the governors in public service, provided those images include clear reuse terms and attribution.

Thank you for helping us preserve and present the history of the U.S. Virgin Islands with accuracy, dignity, and proper credit.

Sincerely,

Ovando Rawlins
VI Guide

## Institution-specific request template

Subject: Request for heritage image and reproduction permission — [Person or institution]

Dear [Institution or family representative],

VI Guide is developing a locally centered heritage experience focused on people whose service and contributions helped shape the U.S. Virgin Islands.

We are preparing a profile of [Name] and would like to request a high-resolution image and permission to reproduce it in VI Guide's public web and mobile experience.

We are especially interested in:

- a primary portrait;
- one contextual image connected to the person's work or community;
- the photographer or creator name;
- the date and event or place shown;
- the preferred credit line;
- confirmation of the rights holder;
- any restrictions on cropping, resizing, educational use, or commercial display.

VI Guide will store and display the source, attribution, contributor information, rights status, and permission record. We will not publish an image as approved until the rights and credit requirements are documented.

Please let us know whether you can provide an approved image directly, identify the proper rights holder, or direct us to the appropriate collection.

Thank you for helping preserve and share this Virgin Islands story.

Sincerely,

Ovando Rawlins
VI Guide

## Intake checklist

For every received image:

- assign image ID
- record entity ID and entity type
- preserve original filename
- record institution or contributor
- record creator or photographer
- record original date, if known
- record source page and collection identifier
- save written permission or license evidence
- record required credit line
- record crop and derivative restrictions
- preserve original-resolution master
- create app-ready WebP or JPEG derivative
- create thumbnail
- review identity and caption accuracy
- update `data/heritage/image-curation.json`
- mark `approved` only after rights review

## Publication gate

An image may enter the live app only when all of the following are present:

- verified subject identity
- verified source
- rights status
- credit line
- permission or license evidence
- image quality suitable for the intended placement
- editorial review
