# USVI tourism photo licensing note

As of 2026-08-08, VisitUSVI's public copyright statement says images on the site are reprinted by permission and unauthorized reproduction is prohibited. The Preferred Partner program describes professional visual assets as USVI-owned and distributed to participating businesses, but the public website does not publish a third-party reuse license for those assets.

For VI Guide, do not copy or hotlink VisitUSVI/partner photography into production unless we have one of the following:

- written permission from the U.S. Virgin Islands Department of Tourism or the applicable rights holder;
- an explicit public license covering VI Guide's commercial use; or
- a locally stored replacement whose reuse rights are documented.

Preferred replacement target for the homepage Ride card: a professional enclosed USVI taxi van (Ford E-Series/Transit style), ideally sourced directly from the Department of Tourism's media/Preferred Partner library with written permission and attribution requirements recorded alongside the asset.
