# Island Journey product contract

The traveler should experience inter-island movement as one VI Guide journey, not a collection of disconnected tools.

For this slice:

- Ferry schedule truth comes from the governed Ferry Planner.
- Ground legs always continue through VI Guide Mobility with context.
- Whole-journey help always continues through VI Concierge with context.
- Itinerary editing remains Planner.
- The canonical saved traveler command center remains My Trip at `/trips`.
- The UI must explain check-in buffers and schedule variability without implying real-time ferry availability.
